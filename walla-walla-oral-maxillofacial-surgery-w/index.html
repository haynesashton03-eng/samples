OMS Procedures

Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery is a specialty in the field of Dentistry. It requires a minimum of 4 years of comprehensive surgical and anesthesia training after dental school.

Wisdom Teeth

Evaluation and removal of wisdom teeth, with surgical instructions to guide your recovery at home.

Dental Implants

A full range of dental implant procedures. Implant patients start with a consultation before the procedure appointment.

Orthognathic Surgery

Corrective jaw surgery to address problems with how the jaws and teeth fit together.

Bone Grafting

Rebuilding bone where it's needed, often to prepare for dental implants.

Facial Trauma

Treatment of jaw and facial injuries, provided by our surgeons in the hospital setting.

Pathology

Diagnosis and treatment of problems affecting the mouth, teeth and face.

Anesthesia

Oral and maxillofacial surgeons complete comprehensive anesthesia training as part of their specialty.

Preparing for your visit

Your first visit may be a diagnostic exam to discuss treatment options, and it often takes place at the procedure appointment. Implant cases and patients with extensive medical histories have a consultation first.

Please bring

  • Your surgical referral request and any X-rays
  • A list of the medications you currently take
  • Completed insurance forms and your policy information, if you have medical or dental insurance
  • A parent or guardian, for all patients under 18

Let us know about

  • Medical conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or artificial heart valves or joints
  • Medications such as heart medications, bisphosphonates, aspirin or blood thinners

Insurance & payment

Fee estimate

We provide an estimate after our surgeons review your X-rays, or at your initial consultation.

We bill your insurance

Our staff can bill your carriers and request predetermination or preauthorization for planned procedures.

Payment

Payment is due in full at the time services are rendered unless other arrangements are made in advance. Credit cards accepted.

For referring doctors

Send a referral and any X-rays you've taken. If time is short, patients can pick up their films and bring them along. We can take additional films if needed.
