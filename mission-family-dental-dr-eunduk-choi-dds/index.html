Dr. Eunduk Choi, DDS

General & Family Dentistry

Dr. Choi graduated from Loma Linda University School of Dentistry. She is a member of Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society and was awarded the Ben W. Oesterling award for “Outstanding Ability in Removable Prosthetics” as well as being recognized by the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID) for excellence in implant dentistry knowledge.

Dr. Choi is an active member in the American Dental Association and its state and local chapters, the Washington State Dental Association and the Walla Walla Valley Dental Society. She also holds membership in the Academy of General Dentistry and values it for the outstanding professional continuing education opportunities it provides.

  • Loma Linda University School of Dentistry graduate
  • Member, Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society
  • Member, Washington State Dental Association & Walla Walla Valley Dental Society
