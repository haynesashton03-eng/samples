About D'LUX

One shop for the whole family

D'LUX Barber & Beauty describes itself as the only dual barber and beauty shop of its kind in the Hill Country. Book a fade and a color appointment at the same address on Junction Hwy.

The shop is owned and run by Candice "Candi" Cathey, a barber, cosmetologist and business owner who focuses on quality cuts and color made for your own style.

D'LUX carries L'ANZA hair care and puts hair health first, with treatments in the chair and advice on the products and routine to keep your style looking good at home.

  • Sunday walk-insHaircuts only. Check in on the walk-in list; no chemical services on Sundays.
