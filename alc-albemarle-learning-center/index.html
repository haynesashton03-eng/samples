Getting children ready for Kindergarten

School readiness means that children are socially, emotionally, physically and cognitively prepared for continued success in Kindergarten. Your child will enjoy a variety of learning experiences, engaged in activities designed to help them develop and soar.

Ages 0 to 5

Our philosophy

“Where all Flowers Bloom!”

At ALC, children learn through play, and every activity is meant to teach something. We want children of all abilities to grow, feel loved, and develop at their own pace.

Our approach follows a structured early-childhood curriculum and draws on Howard Gardner’s theory of multiple intelligences: the idea that every child learns in their own way.
