A home-grown plumbing company

L&B Plumbing is a full service, family-owned plumbing company located in Stillwater, OK. We opened our doors in 2017 to help meet the high demand of plumbing service in the north central Oklahoma area.

Brian Rickman has been working in the plumbing industry since 2006. He started his career doing residential repairs and worked his way up, and by 2015 he was installing plumbing for hotels all around the state. After working for various companies, Brian decided to start his own business with his wife, Laura.
